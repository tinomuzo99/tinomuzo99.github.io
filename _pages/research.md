# Research

My research focuses on how AI systems interpret user intent, how that interpretation changes during conversation, and what counts as convincing evidence that a model understands the user.

## 1. User intent in conversational recommender systems

How can conversational systems infer implicit goals and preferences from dialogue, represent them, and update them as a conversation evolves?

## 2. Evaluation of LLM-based personalization

Recommendation accuracy and dialogue quality do not necessarily establish whether a system inferred the user's actual intent. I study evaluation methods, metrics, and reference standards that make this distinction explicit.

## 3. Consistency and reproducibility

How stable are inferred intents across repeated model runs, equivalent user expressions, and evolving conversations? What experimental reporting practices support reproducibility?

## 4. Applied machine learning and optimisation

Outside my central PhD topic, I develop data-driven decision-support tools using forecasting, optimisation, and reproducible software workflows.

### Current research

**Systematic review (in progress):** Evaluation of LLM-based user-intent inference and tracking in conversational recommender systems, with emphasis on accuracy, consistency, and reproducibility.
