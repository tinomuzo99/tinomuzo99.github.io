# About me

I am a joint PhD researcher at **Stellenbosch University** and the **University of Groningen**, working at the intersection of conversational recommender systems, large language models, and user-intent modelling. My research examines how systems infer and track what users want, and how we can evaluate the **accuracy, consistency, and reproducibility** of that inference.

My background is in Industrial Engineering and Data Science, with interests in applied machine learning, natural language processing, optimisation, recommender systems, and responsible AI. I hold an MEng in Industrial Engineering, with a Data Science focus, from Stellenbosch University.

I am interested in research that turns reliable machine learning methods into useful, testable decision-support systems.

## Research interests

- Conversational recommender systems and personalization
- Large language models and natural language processing
- User-intent inference and tracking
- Evaluation, robustness, consistency, and reproducibility
- Optimisation and applied machine learning
